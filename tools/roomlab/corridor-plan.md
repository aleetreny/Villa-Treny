# Corridor pass — reference-only plan

The consolidated habitat has eleven dedicated corridor visuals to finish. None
will introduce a new room or require pixel art drawn from scratch. Every visible
piece must be copied from the ZIP sheets already used by its endpoint rooms.

| Corridor | Current place in the map | Existing visual vocabulary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Climb | Bridge ↔ Dock | Office/Shelter ladders, steps and hull plate |
| The Long Walk | Through the five Cabins | Existing cabin shell, lamps and corrugated plate |
| The Throat | Infirmary ↔ Workshops | Medical hull plate changing into Workshop block and floor |
| The Landing | Workshops ↔ Common | Workshop apron, crates and worn shared-room floor |
| The Cut | Common ↔ Administration | Damaged Office door/wall pieces and Common floor |
| The Green Run | Hydroponics Yard ↔ Shelter Entrance | Exact Garden soil, planters and Shelter exterior ground |
| The Row | Through the six Diggings | Existing digging shell, metal patches and door fronts |
| The Drop | Workshops ↔ Well | Workshop grating, bathroom pipework and wet floor |
| The Pipe Run | Row ↔ Well | Bathroom pipes and the narrow digging-wall vocabulary |
| The Long Gallery | Row ↔ The Face | Shelter terrain, track and excavation debris |
| The Break | Inside the far edge of The Face; not a traversable room link | Exact broken-wall and exposed-rock pieces already used by The Breach |

The Surface Path, Shelter Door, Descent, Paper Run, East Gallery, Old Wing and
Service Run are labels for short sequences of ordinary door-to-door links. They
do not need separate room assets: their adjoining approved room packs already
contain the required doors, floors and wall transitions.
