# Design System: Villa Treny

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "A question. Six lives."**

Villa Treny extends the approved Night Shift world into a quiet place to read. Dark iron surfaces, warm paper, restrained brass and moss, local pixel lettering and the original handmade portraits connect the conversation to its habitat. The interface is direct and settled: visible words, ordinary links, square controls and enough space for sustained reading. Product copy is English.

The daily forum, archive and six resident profiles now establish the primary visual patterns. The pixel habitat remains a separate surface with its approved room art and movement. The former 25-person observer is recoverable at `?legacy=1`; its journal, economic ledger and relationship graph are historical patterns, not the current forum's page structure. Portfolio and its original Night Shift mode retain their own design authority.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Pixel lettering identifies places and people; condensed prose carries long arguments.
- Warm text and fine structural rules sit on flat, dark iron surfaces.
- Portraits remain authored pixels, scaled in whole multiples without smoothing.
- Reading links, source quotations and restrained selected states make navigation legible.
- The habitat preserves the original rooms, exact crops, collisions and ambient movement.

The implementation evidence is `src/styles/debate.css`, `ForumApp.tsx`, `Rooms.tsx`, the inherited habitat styles and renderers, and the fourteen desktop/mobile captures in `.impeccable/review/`. Those captures use local evaluation text; they do not establish public publication status. Page strategy remains in `.impeccable/daily-forum-brief.md` and the emitted contract in `index.html`.

## Colors

The palette retains the Night Shift iron, paper, brass and moss character. The frontmatter records the actual CSS values, including the slight component-specific tones already visible in the built forum.

### Primary

- **Brass** (`brass`) carries links and navigation. **Light brass** (`brass-hover`) marks hover; **focus brass** (`brass-focus`) defines the keyboard outline.
- **Warm title papers** (`brand-paper`, `headline-paper`, `current-edition-paper`) distinguish the masthead, case title and selected recent discussion without adding another accent family.
- **Question brass** and **person brass** distinguish the central question and author links from continuous prose.

### Secondary

- **Moss** marks source permalinks and pressed control borders. The selected background and selected text form a subdued green state, including reading view and a saved recommendation.
- **Profile moss**, **quotation moss** and **subject moss** are existing local variations for profile labels, reply quotations and archive subjects. They are not separate semantic status categories.
- **Habitat brass**, **habitat muted** and **habitat clay** preserve the inherited room interface and the historical observer's state vocabulary. Do not recolor room pixels to match the UI palette.

### Neutral

- **Ground**, **masthead**, **sidebar** and **control** provide the principal dark surface steps. **Paper** is continuous text; **muted** is metadata and supporting copy. **Habitat note** is the small room explanation.
- **Summary**, **target** and **notice** are intentional contextual surface changes. Each has an established edge or outline; none introduces a floating card treatment.
- The named **edge** tokens retain the implemented border hierarchy: quiet recent/post rows, stronger round/profile starts, moss quotation/summary edges and warmer notice/masthead edges. Reuse the appropriate role when extending a component rather than adding another near-duplicate gray.
- Portraits retain their inherited dark ground and faint cool border; this narrow legacy frame is not a new cyan interface accent. Scrollbars use their existing subdued thumb and track.

**The Source Pixels Rule.** Interface tokens style the surrounding controls and text. The palette, alpha and detail inside approved room images and authored portraits remain governed by their source evidence.

The sidecar's eight-step OKLCH strips are generated swatch previews. They are not additional approved interface colors; the extracted frontmatter values remain normative.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Pixelify Sans, locally served from `public/fonts/PixelifySans.ttf`. Forum headings retain their sans-serif fallback; the inherited atlas keeps its monospace fallback.

**Body Font:** IBM Plex Sans Condensed, locally served in regular and semibold. It carries paragraphs, the question, labels, links, navigation and fields. IBM Plex Mono remains available for the historical observer's clock; it is not the forum's reading font.

The implemented ramp is contextual rather than a single mathematical ratio. Pixelify conveys the existing game identity in short labels and headings. Condensed prose uses open leading, ordinary mixed case and restrained emphasis.

### Hierarchy

- **Display:** the case title uses `display`, changing to `display-mobile` at 760 px. Empty-board headings also use 40 px, with 1.2 leading.
- **Headline and masthead:** archive/profile page headings use `headline`; the brand uses `masthead` and `masthead-mobile`. Their sizes are independent of the case title.
- **Titles:** archive/summary titles use `title`; profile names use `person-title`; conversation sections use `section`. The room, roster, rail and atlas keep their documented roles.
- **Question:** semibold condensed `question` changes to `question-mobile`. Its maximum measure is 58 ch.
- **Reading:** posts use `post` with a 72 ch maximum and 18 px paragraph separation. Posts are 19 px, increasing to 21 px at 1800 px. Reading view uses 20 px / 78 ch on desktop and 19 px on mobile. Full scenario copy uses `scenario`, also changing to 19 px on mobile. Main interface and summary copy uses `body`.
- **Supporting reading:** profile introductions and definitions use 17 px with their 1.5/1.55 leading; quotations use `quotation`. Status notices use `notice`. Profile definitions stop at 65 ch; archive summaries and edition notes stop at 75 ch.
- **Labels:** the 12–15 px steps serve dates, footer, explanatory text and controls, never the long debate body. A 16 px navigation step applies on mobile. Labels have no added uppercase tracking; subjects use capitalization only.

**The Reading Voice Rule.** Keep long arguments and scenario paragraphs in IBM Plex Sans Condensed. Pixelify belongs to short headings, names and place labels.

## Layout

The forum is a full-width scrolling document. Shared gutters use `clamp(16px, 2.5vw, 48px)`, with safe-area insets for notched devices. The shell no longer has a fixed centered outer width. Long prose deliberately retains a readable measure rather than stretching across a large monitor. The desktop masthead is at least 109 px high. The board rail varies from 206 to 280 px; author columns vary from 130 to 185 px and sit beside a flexible argument column.

Archive and resident pages use the same fluid gutters. Filters give spare width to search while retaining native subject/sort selectors. Profile sheets use a responsive grid with a 420 px target minimum; at 1800 px they show three columns. A single profile remains a bounded 1050 px sheet. No decorative cards or new artwork were introduced.

### Responsive behavior

- **At 1100 px and below:** the secondary schedule disappears, the board rail becomes 200 px and author information moves above arguments. The habitat roster moves below the room in three columns.
- **At 760 px and below:** the four main destinations fit in one visible row, each at least 48 px high. The board rail disappears. Posts, archive entries and profiles use one column. Filters wrap into a search row and two native selectors. Controls and inputs are at least 44 px high on touch screens; inputs use at least 16 px type.
- **Reading view:** removes the masthead, rail and footer. A sticky bar keeps Exit reading view and reading navigation available at every scroll position. Escape also exits. Linked quotations/summary arguments focus their destination and expose a return to the reply, summary or question. Copy link uses a dated canonical URL and provides visible success or a selectable fallback.

The habitat uses a 175–250 px atlas, a flexible room and a 210–280 px roster on large screens. At 1100 px the roster moves below; at 760 px the atlas becomes a compact orientation preview next to a native room selector. Its tiny map targets are not interactive on touch/narrow layouts. Mobile roster entries retain generous portrait, name, current room and follow controls; following scrolls the resulting room into view. Stopping follow retains the resident's current room. Room and follow choices participate in browser history.

The stage derives its height from the viewport (400–700 px on portrait mobile), while short landscape screens retain a scrollable usable room. Only the camera pans artwork; the document never scrolls horizontally. Camera magnification stays integer 1×–5× and sprite buttons retain transparent 24×44 native geometry regardless of generic forum/legacy button styles. Touch zoom buttons are at least 44×44 CSS pixels.

## Elevation & Depth

The current forum uses flat surfaces, fine rules and small tonal changes. It has no box-shadow or glass vocabulary. The summary is a moss-dark bounded section; a linked post receives a dark target background and a warm one-pixel outline. Approved room art and foreground masks carry the habitat's spatial depth.

The historical observer retains a small connection-popover shadow in its own stylesheet. That legacy exception does not become a forum card or modal style. Post target highlighting transitions over 300 ms with `ease-out` only when reduced motion is not requested. The inherited room fade is 180 ms with `ease-out`; reduced motion disables it. Text is present immediately.

**The Flat Surface Rule.** Establish reading hierarchy through spacing, type, rules and the existing surface tones. Keep shadows out of new forum components.

## Shapes

Controls, fields, summaries and portrait frames have square corners. Most structure uses one-pixel lines; the active navigation underline and keyboard focus outline use two pixels. Links remain text, with visible underlines where they serve reading navigation. The habitat atlas retains its irregular cluster of rectangles: selected rooms have a brass contour, sealed Breach has a dashed edge, and small marks show occupancy. The atlas indexes room families; its geometry does not assert physical travel distances.

## Components

### Buttons and recommendations

Square, compact and textual. The ordinary control uses `button`, an edge border and a minimum height of 42 px. Hover changes background, border and text without a lift or shadow. Pressed controls use the selected/moss state; disabled controls use 0.6 opacity and a default cursor. Text buttons remove background and border and retain an underline. Global forum focus is a two-pixel brass outline with a five-pixel offset.

Expand board uses 14 px type and a 38 px desktop / 44 px mobile minimum height. The persistent reading controls are at least 44 px high. The recommendation uses a simple inline bookmark, text and a count separated by a rule. Its wording is **Worth reading** / **Recommended**, with `aria-pressed`, visible saving/error feedback and a reversible saved state. It recommends the whole discussion; do not turn it into a resident score or winner badge.

### Inputs and navigation

Fields are solid dark rectangles with an edge border, 8 px by 12 px padding, a 42 px minimum height, paper text, muted placeholders and a brass caret. Every archive input/select has a visible label. Navigation uses muted text, warm active text and a brass underline for `aria-current="page"`; the mobile row remains available without a hamburger replacement. The skip link is visible on focus.

### Scenario disclosure, posts and summary

The case title and central question remain visible. The **Read the full scenario** disclosure follows the reading links; its body contains the full context and a nested **What the case establishes** disclosure. Both are native keyboard-operable details/summary elements with structural rules. The outer disclosure uses 17 px text, paper content and a brass summary; nested facts use the smaller muted treatment.

Posts are ruled rows with an identity area, author profile link, lens, round label and explicit Copy link action. Opening thoughts and Across the table separate the rounds. Reply quotations use moss text and horizontal rules; each links to its actual source post. Target styling, programmatic focus and a persistent return control mark the destination without a modal. An opening-round label is plain text; a reply-round link goes to the other resident’s actual opening rather than targeting itself. Where they landed uses the single bounded `summary` surface, wraps source links below each point and links to the relevant arguments.

### Archive and resident sheets

Archive entries are linked pixel titles, ordinary summaries and secondary dates, subjects and recommendation totals. Filters, pagination, empty results and connection errors use the same quiet form vocabulary. The subject label is plain metadata, not a pill or chip.

The six resident sheets preserve the original portrait and name, followed by the current lens, introduction and a habitat link. Definitions use short moss labels above readable prose. Portraits are authored on a 24 × 26 native canvas: 2× in the board/room roster, 4× in desktop profiles and 3× in mobile profiles. The larger profile is a full page, not the historical observer's notebook overlay.

### Habitat and retained artwork

Only the chosen room renders. RoomAtlas keeps 45 visitable interiors and the sealed Breach reference; Long Walk and Row remain recoverable in the underlying room model and RoomLab and resolve to Common in this observer. RoomScene preserves exact approved pixels, alpha masks and foreground occlusion. Its zoom is integer 1×–5× with Fit and scrolling; room and resident magnify together with nearest-neighbour rendering.

Residents use the existing 24 × 44 native bodies, calibrated against 28 × 62 beds and 26 × 63 lockers. Faces are exact 2:1 reductions of authored portraits; feet reserve 12 × 8 pixels. Movement respects artwork clearance, the walkable mask, route segments and other reservations. A full scene keeps waiting residents in its state rather than drawing overlapping bodies. Follow tracks a resident; manual room selection cancels follow. Pause and reduced motion stop ambient walking. The native six-pixel in-scene name label enlarges with the room and is not a general UI type step.

Artwork authority and reproducible exports are documented in `public/habitat/README.md`, `tools/roomlab/explorer-room-art-sources.md`, its source manifests, and `tools/roomlab/canonical-legacy/manifest.json`. Keep original crop coordinates and authored portrait specifications. Rooms, portraits and bodies have distinct provenance: the handmade character layer is not a sprite extracted from the licensed room packs. Existing JPEG-source limitations remain explicit. Ship finished room images and licensed fonts. The owner confirmed permission for this public source repository on 9 September 2026; original authors’ licence notices remain intact and the application bundle still excludes raw packs.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- Do preserve the approved Night Shift artwork, local fonts and iron/paper/brass/moss character.
- Do keep the question and arguments readable, with ordinary links back to profiles and source posts.
- Do use the documented control states, visible labels, keyboard focus and reduced-motion behavior.
- Do treat recommendations as a reversible signal that the discussion is worth reading.
- Do preserve exact source crops, native scale, alpha masks, foreground depth and collision reservations in the habitat.
- Do keep historical observer rules and Portfolio's independent identity scoped to their own surfaces.

### Don't:

- Don't replace approved rooms or portraits with generated art, gradients or invented furniture.
- Don't use pixel type for long paragraphs or decorative monospaced technical labels in the forum.
- Don't introduce neon, glow, glass, rounded pills or raised repeated cards into this pinned visual world.
- Don't let visual walking, follow, pause or room selection determine the daily arguments.
- Don't present local review text, missing data or an incomplete edition as a completed published discussion.
- Don't restore the old 25-person notebook, economy or relationship graph as the current forum's primary layout.
