# Open-message correction: proposals return, acceptance does not

Six local responses applied. **Five explicit proposals appeared**, including four successive work proposals in the Lior/Juno conversation. None was accepted: three were replaced and the last remained open. The last three Lior/Juno proposals had identical parties, work, price and deadline. No agreement or payment occurred. Lior did perform one repair through his own plan during the subsequent offline watch; that work was not contractual.

This is an assistant-authored review of all six outputs and exact persisted effects, not a human/expert evaluation or a demonstration of general economic autonomy. The [original report](report.json) and [ledger](ledger.json) remain intact.

## Material experimental limitation

After all six calls had completed, a peer found a contradictory advisory description: the supplied deal schema still said `Requires a message to your counterpart with close:false`, while the new protocol4 SYSTEM and enforced grammar allowed omission. The outputs contained five deals without `close` despite that description. The experiment demonstrates that these proposals were reachable and locally valid; it is **not a clean, internally consistent prompt intervention**. No result was repaired and no remaining request was rerun. The description is retained in every original payload and the frozen source bundle.

The three matched cases also changed **two intended variables** from V7: the SYSTEM became the current protocol4 instructions and the schema became corrected protocol4. USER, original before-state and sampling stayed fixed. Therefore even without the discovered contradiction, differences cannot be attributed solely to the grammar change.
