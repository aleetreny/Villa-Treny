# Required offer disposition: a tested precedent, with a narrower open hypothesis

The archive already contains a compulsory economic-choice experiment. It would be incorrect to call that broad idea new or to describe every previous choice-structure experiment as a failure. No new code or inference accompanies this review.

## What P5 actually tested

The [six-call P4/P5 comparison](model-society-ordered-choice-local-2026-09-08/review.md) used three selected J/L/J states. Every P5 response had a required `turn` containing exactly two elements: an explicit economic `choice`, then content. `no_deal` remained legal; exact `accept:<offerId>` and `reject:<offerId>` literals and proposal variants were available. The archived [ledger](model-society-ordered-choice-local-2026-09-08/ledger.json) records P5 accepting offer70,75 and79 while the paired P4 outputs proposed work again. All six applied. Three alternative zero-cell agreements were created, with no physical watch, progress unit or payment. The first two P4 counterproposals changed deadlines legitimately; acceptance was not required for success.

This changed both the compulsory choice representation and its placement/instructions. It did **not** isolate mandatory choice from ordering, naming or instruction changes. It is also distinct from the [earlier six-call property-order probe](model-society-v7-deal-order-local-2026-09-08/review.md), whose outputs were unchanged. Repeating property reordering alone is not the proposed next step.

## Why that requirement disappeared

The [original records proposal](records-capability-proposal-2026-09-08.md) planned to retain ordered decision/content. The later [P6 measurement checkpoint](records-protocol6-measurements-2026-09-08.md) explicitly selected the P4 object with optional `record`, **without P5's tuple**, to limit the simultaneous change while evaluating records. It acknowledges that the first remote P5 job eventually applied after two rejections and feedback; the [provider-status record](ordered-choice-provider-status-2026-09-08.md) does not establish tuple incompatibility. No recorded experiment proves that dropping compulsory choice was necessary for tokens or semantic quality.

The initial frozen P6 source bundle, captured on8 September at18:13:12.572Z, has SHA256 `7fd99b643f7d0486d9c2fadd57ac1ef7f2decb909494bfcbfe2e01feaf3aba55`; its81 text hashes were independently verified. In that bundle:

- `record-instructions.ts:3–6` explicitly documents the deliberate P4-object selection.
- `record-choice.ts:102–104` derives P6 from `proposalCapabilityChoiceJsonSchema`; lines154–161 validate/decode that object.
- `society-protocol.ts:12–13` preserves separate P5 ordered and P6 record handlers; the scheduler selects ordered only for version5.

Thus P6 did not reinterpret saved P5 jobs or accidentally stop accepting their version. It deliberately selected a different producer contract. All ten initial archived P6 request schemas accept their original object output and reject a P5 `turn` wrapper; nine real responses applied. The [P6 local review](records-protocol6-local-review-2026-09-08.md) retains eight proposals and zero acceptance, without claiming that contract selection alone caused the result.

P7 subsequently composes the P6 record contract and adds exclusive retrieval (`retrieval-choice.ts:63–89`). P8 composes the same record contract inside the chosen attention branch (`attention-choice.ts:121` and236), while keeping lookup exclusive. Attention choice is compulsory in P8, but economic disposition is still optional. The nine-entry [current comparison](attention-chain-mechanical-review-2026-09-09.md) is consistent with that distinction; it cannot establish causality.

## Next milestone, design only

The narrower untested hypothesis is that an **explicit disposition of the actual incoming offer when replying** reduces speech/action mismatch under current P8, while preserving legitimate deferral. Reuse the proven distinction `no_deal` / `accept:ID` / `reject:ID` and the existing proposal forms; `no_deal` means no economic operation and remains a valid way to defer. A counterproposal must use real terms and the existing proposal authority, rather than inventing agreement. Accept/reject IDs come only from the selected channel's issued incoming offers. No penalty, forced closing, compulsory agreement, expiry extension or extra inference follows from deferring.

Keep P8 attention, private work, record operations, exclusive lookup, exact grants and current evidence. The requirement must not force a resident doing unrelated private work or retrieval to address another channel. Use a newly versioned producer/adapter if later selected; preserve the complete saved P1–P8 dispatch paths. A format-only explicit `no_deal` maps to today's absence of a deal and creates no additional world state or migration by itself.

Before selection, decide whether to restore this P5 distinction inside P8, validate equivalent canonical effects, and measure complete Groq admission for all25 actual residents plus the legal concurrent-channel/record stress cases. Before any behavioral claim, plan a matched **optional versus required disposition** ablation with the same selector names, object placement, context and instructions. Changing only whether that selector is required separates this hypothesis from another ordering intervention; comparing it directly with a differently worded tuple would not. Keep exact refusal, counterproposal, deferral and phase-fidelity outcomes separate from acceptance counts. Historical P5 supports considering this distinction but does not validate it under current P8.

If that future study assesses performance, predeclare native watches through the original deadline inclusively.
