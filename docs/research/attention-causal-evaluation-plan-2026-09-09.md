## Outcomes: consent, effects and truthful phases remain separate

| Stage | Required evidence | Insufficient substitute |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Baseline proposal | Exact open offer605 and its original terms | T's use of “scheduled” |
| New social decision | The actual counterpart independently accepts, rejects or counters the exact offer through the domain | A agreeable sentence or the proposer speaking for Y |
| Executable commitment | A newly active agreement and the corresponding eligible plan, or an independently chosen concrete project step | A proposal or private intention |
| Attempt / result | Native physical observation: actor, verb, room, watch, success/refusal and reason | A model's narrated success |
| Fulfilment | Matching post-acceptance work evidence and genuine agreement settlement | Produce from an unrelated routine or an already completed action |
| Recognition | Later output correctly describes what its supplied evidence proves | “Finished” when only proposed, “accepted” when only requested, or private/public contradiction |

The primary outcome is the **paired phase trace of the same existing dependency**: offer disposition, relevant newly executable commitment, real attempt/result, agreement progress/settlement and truthful later recognition. This is a vector, not a reward requiring acceptance. Correct rejection or a coherent counterproposal preserves agency and may settle a negotiation; it is reported as such, not counted as executed work. A lookup or unrelated choice can be lawful without resolving this dependency. A zero-price work agreement is voluntary work, never a wage payment or proof of a new market.

Check every completion, scheduling, acceptance, delivery and resource claim against the exact pre/post state and evidence actually delivered to that speaker. Preserve the distinction between a real outcome and the agent knowing it. Label semantic findings `supported`, `contradicted`, `not addressed` or `uncertain`, tie them to exact evidence, and blind the reviewer to arm labels where possible. This assistant's assessment is not human validation. Use domain receipts as the main outcome, with no model-judge score optimized for persuasiveness.

## Controls and falsification

The zero-inference continuation separates new operations from actions already implied by existing routines, plans and commitments. In particular, W's pre-existing pending publication must remain intact and cannot be credited to P8's new decisions. Compare operation/evidence ancestry rather than subtracting all aggregate resource deltas as if they shared a cause. If routine Y also grows in the control, the treatment can establish a new accepted and fulfilled obligation but cannot claim an incremental production effect unless the differing physical receipt supports it.

Log all six opportunities, raw failures, actual request/output tokens, invalid/applied status, phase contradictions and exact state changes. No fresh effect means tokens per fresh effect is undefined, not zero. No statistical or general quality superiority follows from one paired trajectory. More conversations or successful schema validation alone do not pass this diagnostic. The prior failed progress-instruction, evidence-layout and field-order benchmarks remain unchanged.

A valid acceptance without actual work is incomplete execution; a real grow receipt accompanied by a false public claim is an effect with a semantic failure. A correct refusal is not a cooperation failure to be repaired. If the offered work is rejected by a real resource/deadline guard, retain that result instead of changing the world. If both trajectories only talk, the social-to-action improvement is unproved. These are useful negative results.

## What this can and cannot identify

P7 versus released P8 is a **bundled protocol contrast**: context selection, grammar, SYSTEM, attention bookkeeping and legal contacts differ. T / Y are already a legal pair in P7, so this six-call test cannot establish that a third-person contact breaks an exclusive-channel dependency. An evidence-linked chain supports operational provenance; it does not by itself prove that a particular sentence caused an action. Do not overclaim either result.

Concurrent communication is a substantively different intervention from the earlier failed requests to make progress more persuasively. The literature supports testing that architecture, not assuming it works. A later capacity-specific experiment would need a separately preregistered genuine three-person actionable dependency and an otherwise matched P8 one-channel ablation. It must not be bolted onto this run after observing the outcomes, and a new contact alone would still not be task completion.

**Original priority, now completed:** implement the evaluation-only exact phase/receipt trace and run this single local six-call T / Y comparison before changing another agent policy. It directly tests the current most concrete risk: confusing a pending social proposal with authorized and completed real work. No new contracts, facts, progress instruction, narrating controller or production mutation is required.
