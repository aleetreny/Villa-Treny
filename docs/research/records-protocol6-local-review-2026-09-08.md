## Evidence boundaries

The review distinguishes:

- A valid structured response from a useful response to the actual other person's message.
- A draft from an explicit share and from an immutable publication in a later physical action slot.
- A new proposal from independent acceptance of its exact draftID, hash, audience, deadline and amount.
- A scheduled publication from its actual execution; urgent bodily needs may interrupt it.
- Fulfilled zero-price cooperation from positive money transferred for a publication. A promise or accepted-but-unperformed commission is not payment.
- Authored claims and cited source IDs from independently observed facts. A document is not an enforced rule, a diagnosis, an inventory change or evidence of the truth of its text.

Closed conversations, unavailable speakers, unknown or failed responses and schema fallback warnings stop the affected branch. The evaluator does not retry a spent ID, plant a replacement message, force acceptance or continue until a successful outcome. A replay may apply an already durable response after a crash but never obtains a second model answer for the same ID.

Full source states, prompts, raw responses, private drafts and the ledger remain outside Git with restricted file permissions. Public output uses the canonical public projection for speech and documents; it excludes private goals, reflections and document text. Only grouped counts and public content may be copied into the eventual result review.

## Actual context and a bounded follow-up

All six AU/BK requests had an empty accessible draft list, no shown document offers or agreements, no publication intent, and `omitted.drafts:0`. After A authored a private draft, U still correctly received no accessible draft. That absence was not caused by truncating an otherwise readable artifact. The context does not entitle U to infer that a private draft exists, nor does it substantiate U's claim of making a separate log.

This supports one small clarification of P6 instructions: use `records.drafts` for the exact text currently available; omitted counts may indicate other readable text not shown. A transcript saying sent, attached or read is a claim rather than a grant of access. A private draft changes no inventory and grants nobody else access. If text is needed, ask its author to share the exact revision before claiming to review it. This proposal adds no private-state disclosure, censorship, automatic share, inferred consent or action from prose. Its effect on future model behavior is untested; this run does not establish that an instruction caused, or will eliminate, the observed mistakes.

The CN grammar correction is separate: exclude `publish:true` with a private audience while preserving the domain's existing valid operations. A later correction must not rewrite this frozen result. The wider semantic limitations are not resolved by fixing a schema combination.
