## What the current implementation is intended to do

Twenty-five separate residents retain purposes, remember supplied evidence, exchange their own messages and can negotiate consequences in the existing world. Six-hour physical watches handle needs, resources and work. The queue prioritizes first opportunities, then spaces calls by each provider’s remaining allowance and observed costs, with a three-minute minimum and 30-minute failed-attempt backoff. Replies count as individual reviews. Pure pacing waits create no job or attempted thought; [synthetic pacing evidence](research/cognition-pacing-2026-09-08.md) is separate from production observations. The six-hour individual review interval and 480 daily scheduling opportunities are targets/ceilings, not guaranteed successful coverage. No visitor read invokes a model or advances either an action or a physical watch.

The candidate search skips temporarily unaffordable contexts using a local exclusion set, preserves their stored priority and compares at most 25 candidates against shared quota snapshots. Those snapshots grant no spending authority. Waiting is bounded by the next known physical watch, individual review, failed-attempt backoff or open-conversation expiry, while preserving the provider's own clock and a future three-minute minimum. Conversation expiry produces an observation for each participant and can reduce ongoing context. The scheduling calculation retains dates that cross during asynchronous preparation; otherwise a newly eligible resident could incorrectly wait until a much later quota release. These scheduling paths have local regression fixtures; historical release proofs establish only the versions they name. Later retry-cause and deadline corrections belong to their explicitly linked release checkpoints and remain in current P7. The earlier SQL7 cut does not establish their sustained live behavior.

A prepared turn binds one actor, its mind revision, exact evidence, conversation revision, generation and expiry. Each actor receives its own private context plus public information and actual received claims. Other minds' private goals, memories and feelings are not supplied. The engine owns identifiers, participants, speaking order, money, resources and executable effects. A spoken claim never proves work, creates a document or accepts another person's offer. The latest incoming closing turn can now remain available as `receivedClosure` after the exchange closes, with its exact evidence ID and one review opportunity under ordinary scheduling guards. It is not an active reply obligation, an automatic reopening or proof the recipient read it; the next successful review removes that eligibility. See [architecture](society-architecture.md), [scheduler](../workers/habitat-runtime/src/society-scheduler.ts), [turn authority](../src/lib/habitat/society/turn.ts) and [physical integration tests](../src/lib/habitat/society/watch.test.ts).

## Current protocol, distinct from historical trials

The verified producer selects `society_turn` output-contract **version7**. P7 offers either the existing P6 ordinary object or an exclusive private `lookup` operation; it does not use P5’s experimental ordered tuple. P6 originally extended P4’s object with an optional record operation, which P7 preserves. P4 permits proposals in first contact, while acceptance still requires the other resident’s separate turn. Saved versions 1–6 retain their own dispatchers, and historical evaluations pin their recorded version. The release is verified separately from whether any future model uses those operations well. The [capability adapter](../src/lib/habitat/society/capabilities.ts) preserves canonical engine verbs internally while naming their actual effects in new choices: `accrue_labor_credit`, `walk_room_and_log_visit`, `accrue_two_labor_credits`, `log_stock_register`, `filter_water` and `clean_space`. Generic `observe` is absent from new choices but remains readable in historical plans. Explicit destinations and permanent facility restrictions still apply. Renaming physical capabilities did not add document writing, medical consultation, measurements or another action slot. P6 separately implements bounded authored text; it does not turn that text into verified knowledge.

USER starts with the actor's exact canonical name/ID and the names of currently offered recipients, followed by its actual context. This adds identity labels, not another resident's private information or presumed location. SYSTEM remains a separate message. The raw core prompt now targets **6,500 UTF-8 bytes** before scheduler compaction/identity framing; a final USER above that size carries overflow metadata. Whole lower-priority entries may be omitted with counts; essential continuing commitments are retained. This is not a hard full-request token ceiling. New jobs reserve **1,024 output tokens**.

In the ordinary response branch, a first purpose is required when absent. Its first available opportunity also requires contact; an active reply must address its actual counterpart. Later private reviews may remain silent. This rule creates an introduction opportunity; it is not evidence of spontaneous sociability. Social purposes may retain zero physical steps. Private model context and the observer label completed executable steps `steps_finished`, without establishing the broader prose goal; the persisted status remains compatible. Stored unresolved actions such as destination-free `clean` or legacy `observe` carry `legacyVerb` and an explanation rather than an invented capability effect. Final [context tests](../workers/habitat-runtime/test/society-schema.test.ts) cover this projection without rewriting saved plans.

One optional relative `deal` derives transfer/loan/work parties and dates from the actual conversation or its eligible first recipient. In P4/P6, omitting `message.close` preserves the canonical open conversation, without inferring a proposal or acceptance. Explicit `close:true` with a deal is rejected. Instructions and schema descriptions now agree on this behavior; saved versions 1/2/3 keep their previous rules. Acceptance names a still-open incoming offer, revalidates its exact terms and requires a separate counterpart turn. Proposals expire after two physical watches. No raw model-authored `offer`/`respond` fields pass the new boundary. The old contracts remain available for their persisted jobs/history.

An optional [appraisal](../src/lib/habitat/society/appraisal.ts) can change one of the actor's own directed trust, affection, admiration or resentment axes by ±1 after the latest offered incoming message. It requires that message's exact reference and a private reason of 1–180 characters. It cannot change the other person's feelings, desire, financial debt, resources or consent. Per-conversation participant watermarks prevent repeated assessment, including after memory eviction and at a clamped 0/100 boundary. The reason is an interpretation, not a certified fact. [Appraisal tests](../src/lib/habitat/society/appraisal.test.ts) establish these boundaries. Local V7 applied six own-trust increases; two other appraisals had no effect because their containing response failed. Several reasons overstated what the cited messages established, so nuanced or reliably grounded appraisal quality is not demonstrated.

### Released document, retrieval and diagnostic boundaries

The [record domain](../src/lib/habitat/society/records.ts) uses exact authored title/text, server IDs, hashes and revision bindings. Combined content is limited to 600 UTF-8 bytes, title to 96 bytes and citations to four supplied references. New preparations record `records.draftTextMaxLength:504`, producing a 96-character title / 504-character body schema; absent that marker, saved P6 preparations retain the former 600-character body maximum. The combined and individual byte checks are unchanged. Reserving the title allowance addresses the observed ASCII overflow, while multi-byte text can still require domain rejection; no output is trimmed or repaired. Private drafts stay inaccessible to other residents until an explicit authorized share. `publish:true` schedules an exact draft only with a public or named audience; the corrected grammar rejects private publication as the domain already did. Public references cannot leak private source IDs. Empty accessible lists do not create attachments from transcript claims. The [older-record retrieval gap](research/grounded-receipt-selection-2026-09-08.md#limits-and-adjacent-finding) remains in saved P6 preparations; P7 adds literal search, three-entry authorized pages, exact private focus and explicit clear for retained revisions. The [implementation and durable tests](research/authorized-retrieval-implementation-2026-09-09.md) verify later complete-content delivery without sharing or consuming a conversational turn. Pending delivery survives denied quota, preparation, failure and P6; a matching successful P7 result acknowledges it. This is functional access, not verified understanding or autonomous use. Tests of document access must consider original audience, share rows and publications, not infer inaccessibility from a zero share count alone.

One publication consumes the next eligible physical slot, including a still-unused current-watch slot, after urgency handling. It replaces another action. Separate commissions require a readable exact draft, its hash, distinct author/payer, total amount, audience, deadline and independent acceptance. Older promises and record commissions share reservation checks. Only later recorded delivery can settle payment, once; zero-price fulfillment is unpaid cooperation. No text changes stock, medical truth or a binding rule. The observer's public projection and read-only archive exclude private documents and commission terms.

The active records extension has independent bounded drafts, publications, shares, offers, agreements and per-author intents/cursors. SQL9 preserves the previous cognitive singleton bytes/hash, initializes empty records when reading society codec 1, and archives exact publication/draft pairs. The historical SQL9 complete-cut comparison verifies preservation of physical world codec 4, prior jobs, quota rows and the runtime clock through that migration. Current SQL11 stores society codec 3 with per-resident private query/cursor/focus state, archives the exact previous cognitive row and preserves public DTO version 2. Migration continuity is documented separately from protocol tests; old binaries cannot read codec 3 by stripping its private extension.

Private rejection diagnostics capture failed selected candidates, the issued schema and bounded stage/hash metadata before fallback. They do not relax rejection, refund failed usage or grant retries. The public API exposes no raw diagnostic payload. Retention is 64 rows /16 MiB / seven days, collected on provider writes; candidate text is capped at 64 KiB and schema at 192 KiB. A partial capture is marked incomplete, and no full hash is computed beyond the 4 MiB defensive bound. Original prompt text and arbitrary headers/errors are excluded. Metadata and retained candidate bytes cannot reconstruct unavailable prompts or domain state. [Recovery](recovery.md) explains private backup retention; the [diagnostic proposal](research/rejected-response-diagnostics-2026-09-08.md) remains a historical design note rather than a claim of exact replay from hashes.

The released [structural retry feedback](research/structural-retry-feedback-2026-09-09.md) derives at most four issues / 600 UTF-8 bytes from an exact, complete and privately retained issued-schema rejection. Only known field paths, fixed error categories and bounds may enter a metered retry USER.
